# Micro-accessibility v0.1 — active analysis gate

## What is now frozen

The machine-readable registry contains **10 short-timescale biological systems grouped into 5 dependence clusters** spanning within-genotype sectors, developmental transitions, bud sports and within-species series.

The v0.1 analysis separates four questions:

1. whether mechanistic change packages recur across short-timescale systems;
2. whether that recurrence survives dependence collapse across repeated focal taxa;
3. whether explicit mechanistic state nodes are complete enough for a graph-topology null;
4. whether accepted-species macroevolutionary transition weights are identifiable enough for a micro-to-macro prediction test.

Questions 1–2 are now executable. Questions 3–4 remain gated rather than filled with visible-colour proxies.

## Current result

The system-level recurrence sensitivity is suggestive but not conventionally significant (`P ≈ 0.084`). After collapsing repeated studies into five dependence clusters, the primary recurrence result is null (`P = 1.0`): complete A/F/C/P transition packages are not currently shown to recur across independent clusters.

A separate ascertainment audit shows that literature-coded systems resolve the anthocyanin axis much more often than the other pigment axes (A=8, F=4, C=1, P=3; axis-symmetric permutation `P ≈ 0.0081`). This imbalance weakens after dependence collapse, showing that anthocyanin-focused repeated study of a few focal systems contributes to the apparent recurrence.

## Why this is stricter than the previous synthesis

The earlier directional synthesis showed repeated anthocyanin / competing-branch directions. The new analysis demonstrates that this cannot yet be upgraded to a claim of repeated multivariate molecular packages. It explicitly separates directional recurrence, taxonomic dependence and axis ascertainment.

## Cross-scale result already available

A separate count-controlled macro proxy shows that A and Y states occupy substantially fewer traditional-section backgrounds than expected for their species counts, whereas W does not. Thus short-timescale accessibility is not sufficient for broad macro realization even before branch-specific transition rates are identifiable.

## Current claim boundary

Do not use white/red/yellow labels as mechanistic graph nodes. The primary branch-level micro-to-macro prediction remains untested until source/target biochemical states and admitted nuclear-tree macro transition weights are both available.

## Next gate

Candidate-free public RNA-seq reanalysis is now the active bottleneck. All five dependence clusters have at least one public raw-data target; three already have frozen run manifests, and the remaining high-information targets are `PRJNA981682` for *C. perpetua* plus provider-specific *C. reticulata* datasets. The next analysis must uniformly quantify the predefined A/F/C/P modules, reduce missing axes, then rerun the dependence-aware recurrence null.
