# Paper 1 Chinese-language and grey-literature screen — 2026-08-27

## Purpose

This pass closes a specific gap left by the English-language/high-recall audit: Chinese-indexed journal articles, an identified doctoral thesis, and the earliest Chinese *Camellia* pollination record were screened explicitly against the frozen Paper 1 admission rules.

This is **not** a claim that CNKI or Wanfang were exhaustively exported and screened. It is an indexed-source/thesis pass that makes the recovered Chinese-language evidence and its exclusion logic explicit.

The machine-readable ledger is:

`data/paper1_chinese_grey_literature_screen_v0_1.csv`

## Frozen admission rule

A molecular record can add a new recurrence replicate only if it supplies all of the following:

1. a flower/petal contrast that can be canonically oriented;
2. interpretable direction on at least one frozen A/F/C/P transcript-state axis;
3. an evolutionary/biological background independent of an already represented dependence cluster;
4. auditable public raw RNA-seq if the record is to enter the standardized candidate-free arm.

Relevant functional or biochemical studies that fail these requirements are retained as feasibility/context evidence rather than silently discarded.

## Screened records

Ten Chinese-language, thesis, or directly adjacent records were frozen in this pass.

### Existing *C. nitidissima* programme

Eight records belong to the already represented `CNITIDISSIMA` background:

- Zhou et al. 2011 — CnCHS cloning/expression;
- Zhou et al. 2012 — CnCHI cloning/expression;
- Zhou 2012 — doctoral thesis on *C. nitidissima* flower-colour genes;
- Zhou et al. 2013 — functional CnFLS1, already present in the earlier additional-screen ledger;
- Zhou et al. 2015 — CnF3H cloning/expression;
- Zhou et al. 2015 — CnCHS cloning and tobacco transformation;
- Jiang et al. 2021 — CnF3'H expression and heterologous function;
- Li et al. 2023 — CnbHLH79 developmental expression/regulatory correlation.

These records substantially strengthen the historical depth of molecular work on golden *Camellia*. They do **not** add eight independent yellow origins. Most concern shared upstream genes or regulators outside the frozen module-defining families, and the one direct F-axis functional record (CnFLS1) is already part of the same `CNITIDISSIMA` mechanistic background.

### *C. oleifera* ANR

Huang et al. 2018 cloned and functionally characterized two *C. oleifera* ANR genes. This is genuine P-axis enzymatic feasibility evidence, but the experiment does not define a matched flower-colour contrast or a canonically oriented transcript-state change. It therefore does not enter the recurrence denominator.

### Historical pollination evidence

Wu 1977 identified specialist pollinating bees of cultivated *C. oleifera*, including *Andrena camellia*. Later pollination reviews identify this paper as the starting point of Chinese pollination-ecology literature. It extends the historical ecology context but does not test flower-colour selection and therefore cannot be assigned to any accepted-species colour-transition branch.

## Grey-literature policy frozen by this pass

For Paper 1:

- theses/dissertations may support priority, search completeness, source genealogy, and an existing dependence cluster;
- a thesis is not counted as an additional independent recurrence replicate when it reports the same taxon/programme later represented by journal papers;
- conference abstracts or unarchived summaries do not enter quantitative recurrence without sufficient methods/data to apply the frozen admission rules;
- functional studies without a canonically oriented flower/petal contrast remain feasibility evidence;
- public raw-data requirements for the candidate-free arm are unchanged.

## What remains before the word “systematic” is justified

This pass narrows, but does not eliminate, the systematic-review gap. A PRISMA-style claim would still require:

1. formal CNKI/Wanfang database exports with frozen database-specific query strings, dates and hit counts;
2. deduplication across Chinese and English databases;
3. a complete title/abstract/full-text flow with exclusion reasons for every retrieved record;
4. closed backward/forward citation chasing and an explicit conference/grey-literature stopping rule.

Until then the correct label remains **database-counted, high-recall evidence audit**, not exhaustive systematic review.

## Scientific consequence

No current biological claim is changed. The strengthened interpretation is narrower:

> *Camellia* flower-colour molecular biology has a deeper Chinese candidate-gene and functional history than the journal-facing reference list alone suggests. That history reinforces the need to distinguish mechanistic feasibility and study-programme depth from independent recurrence. Under the frozen independence rule, the added Chinese/grey records strengthen existing mechanistic context but add no new independent A/F/C/P recurrence cluster.
