# Reproductive/sensory niche turnover — current handoff

## Starting `chun` result

The project has two negative/limiting macro results that must be explained rather than ignored:

1. provenance-aware visible A versus W thermal comparisons are non-significant;
2. within the two traditional-section proxies that contain both A and W, A is not more climatically displaced than W.

At the same time, a same-visible-red Camellia pair has different pollinator/sensory functions.

## New problem

> Are recurrent flower-pigment transitions primarily changes in reproductive/sensory niche rather than repeated innovations in abiotic climatic niche?

## New hypothesis

Accessible pigment-network changes can alter pollinator-perceived spectral phenotype and effective pollen-transfer niche while the lineage remains within broadly similar climatic space. Climate may still matter upstream by changing pollinator reliability or phenology.

## Current evidence ceiling

This is a `chun`-generated hypothesis, not a demonstrated genus-wide result. Standardized spectra/UV/effective-pollination evidence is still sparse, and no Camellia branch-specific true reactivation has passed the nuclear-history + retained-pathway + selection-pressure gate.
