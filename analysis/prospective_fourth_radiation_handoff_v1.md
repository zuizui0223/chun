## Stage-B outcome

The first post-freeze Iris mechanism source screen is now closed as **`HOLD_MECHANISM_OBSERVATION_REGIME`**.

The frozen gate required at least 20 directly measured, Stage-A-mappable Iris taxa in a comparable floral molecular observation regime, plus two fine states inside one coarse class with at least five measured taxa in each. The recovered literature does not meet that gate:

- the broad Iris phytochemistry review covers 91 taxa only fragmentarily across organs and reports flower anthocyanins from 17 species; report absence cannot be recoded as molecular absence;
- the largest direct comparative bearded-Iris chemistry study found in the screen contains 11 taxa;
- the direct cross-species functional/metabolomic DFR study contains 4 species;
- other transcriptome/metabolome studies are predominantly one-species colour-form comparisons;
- the 45-individual Iris hybrid anthocyanin study is not a 20-species Stage-A observation regime and cannot be substituted for the frozen species-level gate.

Therefore B1 coarse molecular alignment and B2 residual fine-state molecular alignment were **not computed**. Stage A remains `MIXED`, and the cross-radiation biological count remains unchanged.
