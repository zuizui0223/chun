# Aquilegia cross-level admission v0.1

## Question

Can North American *Aquilegia* enter the current high-level cross-level endpoint:

`VISIBLE_PHENOTYPE_AXIS_TO_MOLECULAR_SUBSPACE_WITHIN_ONE_RADIATION`

with both required axes measured directly across the radiation?

## Axis 1 — pigment depletion

**PASS.**

The 13-species expression panel includes seven A− molecular lineages and directly measures the anthocyanin pathway across species. Down-regulation is restricted to A− lineages and is stronger toward late pathway genes, especially DFR/ANS (`10.1111/j.1365-294X.2006.03114.x`).

## Axis 2 — blue/red hue branch

**HOLD.**

North American *Aquilegia* contains repeated blue↔red evolutionary changes, and flavonoid branching genes `F3'H` / `F3'5'H` are the biologically expected subspace. However, the evidence currently assembled here is not a matched radiation-wide direct expression panel for this hue axis. It consists of pathway-based inference plus limited species-pair/hybrid genetic architecture (`10.1111/evo.14313`).

That is not equivalent to the Iochrominae/Erica cross-species axis mapping.

## Verdict

`FAIL_HOLD_NOT_MATCHED_RADIATION_WIDE_DIRECT_TWO_AXIS_BRIDGE`

Aquilegia is therefore **not counted** as a third phenotype-axis→molecular-subspace radiation.

This is an evidence-resolution failure, not a negative biological result. The pigment-loss evidence remains strong; the missing requirement is matched direct molecular resolution for the hue axis at the radiation scale.

Paper 1 remains unchanged.
