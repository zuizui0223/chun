# Linoideae terminal-colour evidence seed v0.1

## Purpose

Start the original terminal-trait reconstruction from explicit species-level colour evidence while the source-study 113-tip supplementary list is still being recovered.

These rows are **evidence candidates**, not admitted atlas terminal rows. Every row remains `PENDING` until the source taxon can be mapped unambiguously onto the frozen 113-tip phylogenetic sampling frame.

## First species-level evidence

A 2026 taxonomic/ecological study of the *Linum schiedeanum* complex (`10.17129/botsci.3736`) states that the complex comprises 15 New World species characterized principally by yellow flowers and specifically discusses:

- *Linum guatemalense*;
- *Linum mexicanum*;
- *Linum orizabae*.

The paper explicitly ties this complex concept to Rogers 1968, one of the source-study evidence groups already frozen for the Linoideae rebuild.

The three species are therefore entered as `YELLOW` **candidate evidence** with `ATLAS_ADDITION` provenance. They are not used to infer ancestral states yet.

## Taxonomic safeguard

The same 2026 study supports *L. guatemalense* as distinct but indicates that *L. mexicanum* and *L. orizabae* may represent the same taxonomic entity. The atlas therefore must not count the latter two as independent terminal observations until the source phylogeny names and pinned taxonomy are reconciled.

## Why this is useful

The rebuild can proceed in two independent directions:

1. recover the exact 113 target tips from Supplementary Table S1/tree;
2. accumulate species-level colour evidence with frozen provenance.

Only the intersection of those two streams becomes the final terminal matrix. This prevents either the phylogeny or the colour literature from silently redefining the other.

## Admission rule

A seed row may be promoted only when:

- its source name is matched to one of the 113 target tips;
- accepted-name/synonym mapping is documented;
- no unresolved source conflict exists;
- wild flower colour is supported at species level;
- the row is not duplicated through a taxonomic synonym.

## Boundary

Post-Paper-1 only. No Camellia result is changed.
