# Paper 1 journal-escalation web and prior-art audit — 2026-08-28

## Decision

**Do not escalate the current frozen Paper 1 above the *American Journal of Botany* (AJB) route.** The narrow contribution survives a hostile prior-art search, but it survives as a **matched empirical audit in five public *Camellia* systems**, not as a new general theory of evolutionary predictability. The components carrying broad-journal rhetoric—many-to-one phenotype–mechanism mapping, pathway-level convergence, discovery-method ascertainment, resolution-dependent repeatability, and ancestral-event uncertainty—are already established. Recent 2025–2026 studies make that ceiling sharper, not weaker ([Wenzell et al. 2025](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-025-57639-3); [Li and Zhang 2026](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-026-68838-x); [Berger et al. 2026](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2616248123); [Fan et al. 2026](https://doi.org/10.1111/pbi.70442)).

The exact combination not directly pre-empted in this bounded search is:

1. hold a small set of admitted biological systems fixed;
2. enumerate recurrence compatible with incomplete, literature-selected A/F/C/P observations;
3. remeasure those same systems with one prespecified, annotation-driven pathway-wide rule and show contraction of the recurrence identified set;
4. separately show that a topology/coding-robust local visible-colour pattern can survive while no accepted-species branch event survives the stricter cross-coding identity gate.

That is a defensible combination claim. It is **not evidence of universal priority**, a new estimator, or proof that micro-accessibility predicts macroevolutionary transition. The frozen project itself records that the two arms have different units and estimands and that 0/53 accepted nuclear-tree tips have complete defensible A/F/C/P states ([state-identity gate, immutable repository snapshot](https://github.com/zuizui0223/chun/blob/c6d5f4f9b66f2a20ec4b38a3dc1216dad883649a/docs/PAPER1_STATE_IDENTITY_FRAMING_GATE_V0_1.md)).

## Audit scope and method

This is a **bounded, adversarial web audit**, not a PRISMA systematic review and not a bibliometric priority proof. Searches emphasized 2023–2026 primary research and retained older papers only when they directly established a concept that Paper 1 might otherwise overclaim. Query families included repeated evolution plus standardized reanalysis, observation regime, candidate-gene ascertainment, multivariate pathway recurrence, partial identification, robust phylogenetic pattern versus uncertain transitions, flower-colour pathway convergence, and *Camellia* flower-colour evolution. Citation chaining was limited to sources needed to test the exact novelty claims below.

Only official journal pages are used for journal rules. Scientific claims are tied to primary research articles or primary methodological/theoretical papers through DOI links. Searches and official-page checks were completed on **2026-08-28**. Dynamic charges and instructions must be checked again on the day of submission.

## Exact precedent tests

### Test A — same-system observation-regime remeasurement and contraction of recurrence identified sets

**Target design:** the same repeated biological systems are first scored from literature-selected mechanistic observations, then remeasured under one prespecified pathway-wide observation rule; recurrence compatible with unresolved multivariate states is exactly bounded in each regime, and contraction is reported.

**Bounded-search result: not directly pre-empted.** The nearest precedents each omit at least one defining operation:

- Conte et al. stratified reuse estimates by discovery method across published cases, not matched remeasurement of the same systems ([DOI](https://doi.org/10.1098/rspb.2012.2146)).
- Poore et al. used non-candidate QTL mapping across replicated stickleback contrasts, but did not compare literature-selected and standardized observations of those same contrasts or enumerate missing-state bounds ([DOI](https://doi.org/10.1093/evolut/qpac025)).
- Li and Zhang reanalyzed the same archived experimental-evolution datasets under increasingly strict definitions, which is a strong conceptual near-match, but did not contrast literature-selected versus standardized pathway measurements or derive identified sets from unresolved module states ([DOI](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-026-68838-x)).
- Larter et al. and Wheeler et al. applied coordinated pathway measurements, but without a matched pre/post observation-regime comparison or exact incomplete-state bounds ([Larter DOI](https://doi.org/10.1093/molbev/msy117); [Wheeler DOI](https://doi.org/10.1098/rspb.2023.0275)).

**Allowed novelty wording:** “In this bounded set of public *Camellia* systems, holding systems fixed while standardizing A/F/C/P observation contracted the recurrence set permitted by literature-selected measurements.”

**Forbidden wording:** “the first demonstration that observation changes evolutionary repeatability,” “a new theory of partial identification,” or “candidate-free genome-wide discovery.”

### Test B — robust macro pattern with a separate historical event-identity target

**Target design:** one repeated-evolution study first tests a topology/coding-robust realized pattern and then, separately, requires individual branch-event identity to pass a stricter gate; the pattern survives but the shared event set is empty, so branch-specific causation stops.

**Bounded-search result: the narrow operational combination was not directly pre-empted, but the logic is not new.** Cockx et al. is the closest recent example: broad origin conclusions can be robust across many model/coding choices while individual ancestral states remain sensitive ([DOI](https://doi.org/10.1093/sysbio/syaf063)). Landis et al. combines detectable phylogenetic structure with equivocal historical/pollinator trajectory inference in a botanical study ([DOI](https://doi.org/10.3389/fpls.2015.01144)). Ng and Smith jointly estimated repeated red-flower origins and alternate pathways, but did not implement an empty shared-event stop gate ([DOI](https://doi.org/10.1111/nph.13576)). Fan et al. integrates *Camellia* phylogeny and molecular mechanism while proposing a likely ancestral colour; it does not separate a robust local statistic from a deliberately failed cross-coding event-identity target ([DOI](https://doi.org/10.1111/pbi.70442)).

**Allowed novelty wording:** “The analysis separates a topology/coding-robust local pattern from a stricter accepted-species event-identity gate; no event passed both strict and dominant coding, so downstream branch attribution stopped.”

**Forbidden wording:** “the first study to distinguish evolutionary pattern from events” or “proof that transitions are absent.”

## Evidence-backed journal-fit implications

1. **AJB remains the rational first submission.** Its official plant-science scope genuinely spans the molecular and macroevolutionary layers, its Research Article limits match the project's 242-word structured abstract and six-figure architecture, and it does not require the paper to be a field-wide method or theory ([AJB official author guidelines](https://bsapubs.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/hub/journal/15372197/homepage/forauthors); [AJB official journal information](https://bsapubs.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/hub/journal/15372197/homepage/productinformation); [project checklist](https://github.com/zuizui0223/chun/blob/c6d5f4f9b66f2a20ec4b38a3dc1216dad883649a/docs/PAPER1_AJB_V1_0_SUBMISSION_CHECKLIST.md); [project figure manifest](https://github.com/zuizui0223/chun/blob/c6d5f4f9b66f2a20ec4b38a3dc1216dad883649a/data/paper1_main_figure_manifest_v0_2_2.csv)). The pitch should be N6 + N7 + N8, with the negative event gate treated as a result rather than apologized for.

2. ***Evolution* is not made safe by stronger wording.** Its official warning about established phenomena in another taxon applies directly because N1–N5 are pre-empted ([official guidelines](https://academic.oup.com/evolut/pages/author-guidelines)). N6–N8 are real but rest on three anthocyanin clusters, two yellow trajectories, and no robust shared events. Without a general estimator, cross-taxon replication, or taxon-resolved joint molecular/event test, an editor can reasonably classify the manuscript as an application-specific synthesis. Formatting would also require a ≤200-word abstract and double-anonymous rebuild.

3. ***Evolution Letters* is a hard no-go at the current gate.** Its official threshold asks for a substantial broad advance or highly influential framework ([official guidelines](https://academic.oup.com/evlett/pages/author-guidelines)). The current paper explicitly does not provide a joint state-resolution estimator, and recent large-scale repeatability/convergence work occupies the broad conceptual territory ([Li and Zhang 2026](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-026-68838-x); [Berger et al. 2026](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2616248123)).

4. ***New Phytologist* and *The American Naturalist* do not provide a credible escalation shortcut.** The former's desk gate asks for a major timely plant-science advance and now sits beside strong direct flower-colour and *Camellia* competitors ([official guidelines](https://nph.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/hub/journal/14698137/about/author-guidelines); [Wenzell et al. 2025](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-025-57639-3); [Fan et al. 2026](https://doi.org/10.1111/pbi.70442)). The latter asks for conceptual unification that changes organismal-biological thinking; Paper 1 preserves an inferential boundary but does not introduce a general theory ([official instructions](https://www.journals.uchicago.edu/journals/an/instruct)).

5. ***Molecular Ecology* is not a better fit; *Annals of Botany* remains the clean fallback.** The current paper lacks new molecular-population sampling and risks Molecular Ecology's taxon-limited/descriptive screen ([official guidelines](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/page/journal/1365294x/homepage/forauthors.html)). Annals has the necessary plant breadth and can receive the same bounded claims if AJB declines, subject to its compact page format ([official guidelines](https://academic.oup.com/aob/pages/general_instructions)).

## Locked claim ceiling and future escalation condition

The strongest safe submission sentence is:

> In the same admitted public *Camellia* systems, standardized A/F/C/P observation narrows the molecular recurrence permitted by incomplete literature measurement, while a separate accepted-species analysis retains topology/coding-robust local colour structure but identifies no transition branch robust to strict versus dominant wild-colour coding.

Do not add “first,” “predicts,” “causes,” “universal,” or “the same evolutionary state” as if Paper 1 had estimated one joint cross-scale variable.

A future general-evolution escalation would require at least one material scientific addition, not a rhetorical rewrite: a preregistered estimator validated across multiple trait systems; substantially broader same-system observation-regime replication; or taxon-resolved A/F/C/P states paired with branch events that pass the robustness gate, enabling a genuine test of whether molecular accessibility predicts transition probability. Until then, the evidence-backed decision is **AJB first, Annals of Botany fallback, no journal escalation**.
