# Cross-radiation resolution-dependent flower-colour repeatability — v0.1

## Main decision

The post-Camellia programme is no longer organized around finding one second clade that reproduces the complete Camellia design. The stronger and more comprehensive question is:

> **At which biological resolutions is flower-colour evolution predictable across independent angiosperm radiations?**

The current synthesis supports a resolution-dependent candidate rather than a universal colour direction, universal coarse boundary, or universal complete pigment programme.

## Scope now assembled

The synthesis has three nested evidence layers.

1. **Cross-clade atlas:** 11 clades with macro, molecular, ecological and auditability roles frozen in `white_baseline_cross_clade_atlas_v0_1.csv`.
2. **Direct resolution ledger:** 8 systems contributing 16 common-endpoint rows to macro representation or matched phenotype-axis-to-molecular-subspace tests.
3. **Mechanistic recurrence benchmark:** 7 systems spanning exact-regulatory, pathway-module, regulator-class, recurrent-core and heterogeneous-causal-node resolutions.

These layers are not pooled into one scalar effect because their biological units differ.

## Result 1 — macroevolutionary predictability is not concentrated in one coarse state representation

Across Linoideae, Angraecinae and Antirrhineae:

- fine-state phylogenetic organization is supported in **3/3** independent external radiations;
- a strong universal transition direction is supported in **0/3** comparable source-to-tree radiations;
- the preselected biological coarse partition is enriched over exhaustive alternative bipartitions in **0/3** radiations.

Thus repeatable macro structure survives at finer hue, organ-configuration or pigment-class resolution even though neither one direction nor one privileged coarse colour boundary generalizes.

This does not mean that every fine state is homologous or that fine-state structure identifies historical events.

## Result 2 — molecular predictability differs among phenotype dimensions

Three independent radiations now share the same high-level comparison: phenotype axis -> molecular subspace within one radiation.

### Hue / hydroxylation

- **Iochrominae:** anthocyanidin hue maps to a branching-enzyme subspace.
- **Cape Erica:** both yellow/hue-branch taxa use F3'H-level branching while the six white/depletion taxa do not.
- **Petunieae:** the preregistered hue axis prospectively selects `BRANCHING_HUE` across 53 anthocyanin-positive taxa; best-vs-next AICc margin = **11.60282936**.

Current support: **3/3 systems**, including **1/1 prospective component test**.

### Pigment amount / depletion

- **Iochrominae:** pigment presence/intensity maps to a late-pathway coexpression module.
- **Cape Erica:** white/depletion routes disperse across ANS, DFR, UDP-GST and bHLH.
- **Petunieae:** the preregistered raw amount response fails the distinct late/regulatory prediction; its best model does not improve on the null and the best-vs-next margin is only **0.44436806**. A source-method log transform selects `LATE_OUTPUT`, but this sensitivity cannot upgrade the frozen gate.

A fixed output/regulatory localization is therefore supported in **1/3** matched systems, versus hue/branching localization in **3/3**.

This is a descriptive cross-system asymmetry, not a pooled significance test. The three systems are the biological replication units and only Petunieae was prospective for this bridge.

## Result 3 — mechanistic recurrence is hierarchical rather than whole-programme invariant

The seven-system benchmark spans:

- *Ipomoea* — exact-gene/regulatory recurrence;
- Iochrominae — pathway-module recurrence;
- Aquilegia — late-pathway expression recurrence;
- Epimedium — recurrent core module with heterogeneous implementation;
- Petunia — regulator-class recurrence with distinct complete implementations;
- Cape Erica — heterogeneous causal nodes;
- Camellia — transition-class-dependent modular recurrence under candidate-free multivariate remeasurement.

At the frozen evidence standard, the **strongest supported recurrence level is a complete multivariate programme in 0/7 systems**. This does not prove that complete programme replay never occurs; it means none of these admitted systems requires that level to explain its strongest defensible repeatability result.

## Integrated candidate

> **Flower-colour predictability is resolution dependent across radiations: fine phenotype organization and some biochemically specific molecular axes repeat more reliably than shared coarse boundaries, universal transition directions, or complete molecular programmes.**

The candidate has three useful properties:

1. it is broader than Camellia;
2. it is falsifiable at each layer;
3. it preserves heterogeneous evidence units instead of creating a misleading pooled recurrence proportion.

## What is genuinely new versus prior art

The generic statements that character coding matters, visible phenotypes can hide multiple mechanisms, and pathways can converge are prior art and are not novelty claims.

The new empirical candidate is the **cross-radiation alignment of resolution effects across levels**:

- replicated fine-state organization without a shared privileged coarse boundary;
- repeated hue/hydroxylation localization to branching machinery, including a preregistered prospective Petunieae component;
- heterogeneous levels of mechanistic recurrence across independent systems rather than one complete programme.

A dedicated literature-priority audit is still required before any priority wording is allowed.

## Immediate next falsification

Do not search for a single Camellia clone. Instead:

1. add independent hue/branching systems under a prespecified admission rule, with *Ipomoea* as a strong external functional benchmark but not silently relabelled as the same matched-axis design;
2. add amount/depletion systems to test whether late/output-module localization is genuinely less stable;
3. maintain separate prospective and retrospective evidence counts;
4. perform a targeted prior-art audit for the exact cross-radiation resolution-dependent claim;
5. only then choose the general-evolution journal target.

## Boundaries

- No event-for-event micro-to-macro matching is claimed.
- No universal colour direction or ancestral colour is claimed.
- No common numerical recurrence estimator is constructed across heterogeneous units.
- No retrospective system is relabelled prospective.
- Camellia Paper 1 remains scientifically closed and separate; this is a new cross-radiation paper programme.
