# C. perpetua summer molecular replication audit v0.1

## Question

Before interpreting a future winter-summer difference, is the existing summer molecular anchor itself reproducible across independent studies?

## Primary CHUN anchor

Zhu et al. 2024 (`10.1007/s44281-024-00052-5`) sampled three 15-year-old plants in June 2022 across S1-S5 and is the frozen candidate-free `CPERPETUA` source.

CHUN remeasurement gives:

- A down;
- F down;
- C up;
- P down (`OLS_stage_slope = -0.6156`, exact five-stage order P = 0.0167).

## Independent 2023 floral study

Deng et al. 2025 (`10.5511/plantbiotechnology.25.0317a`) independently sampled *C. perpetua* flowers from three similarly sized plants on 23 June 2023 across S1-S4.

Its stage-resolved evidence is not a second CHUN A/F/C/P matrix. The study used the full-length transcriptome primarily for gene discovery, stage RT-qPCR for CpSCPL1A genes, and HPLC for four galloylated catechins.

The independent chemical result is nevertheless informative at the late-bloom boundary:

- CG: S3 peak -> lower at S4;
- EGCG: S3 peak -> lower at S4;
- ECG: rises to S3 -> lower at S4;
- GCG: already declining after S2 -> lower again from S3 to S4.

Thus **4/4 measured galloylated catechins decline from S3 to full-bloom S4**.

## Interpretation

This is a positive partial replication of a functional branch, not a full-signature replication.

The result supports the narrower statement:

> An independent 2023 flower cohort reproduces a late-bloom decline in flavan-3-ol/galloylated-catechin output that is directionally compatible with the candidate-free 2022 P-down trajectory.

It does **not** justify saying that Deng et al. independently recovered A down / F down / C up / P down. Galloylated catechin abundance is not identical to the CHUN P expression/module estimand, and stage-resolved A/F/C/P cannot be reconstructed from the reported Deng measurements under the frozen candidate-free rules.

## Consequence for the seasonal experiment

The summer side is no longer supported only by one publication at the broad functional level: two independent June cohorts show flavonoid/catechin pathway organization, and the late-stage P-like chemical direction agrees.

But this does not fill the critical missing measurement:

**winter mature full-bloom petal A/F/C/P + spectral state in the same tagged GBG population.**

The PR #139/#140/#141/#142/#143 experimental and inferential gates remain unchanged.

## Claim ceiling

Cross-study partial functional replication only. No winter inference, no full A/F/C/P independent replication, and no historical colour-transition causation.
