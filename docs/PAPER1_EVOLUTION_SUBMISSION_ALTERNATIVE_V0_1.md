## Title

**What repeats when evolution reaches similar flower colours in *Camellia*?**

## Abstract

Repeated evolution can refer to recurrence of a phenotype, molecular module, whole programme, phylogenetic pattern, or historical event. We tested which quantities remain repeatable in *Camellia*. Public flower-colour RNA-seq systems were represented on four prespecified A/F/C/P transcript-state axes. In three anthocyanin-gain dependence clusters, standardized remeasurement contracted literature-compatible exact recurrence from 0.333–1.0 to 0.333 and pairwise concordance from 0.333–1.0 to 0.333–0.5. In two yellow-development trajectories, exact recurrence contracted from 0.5–1.0 to 0.5 and pairwise concordance from 0.25–1.0 to 0.75; A, C, and P directions agreed while F differed. Separately, accepted-species wild colours retained robust local nearest-same-colour structure across independent nuclear topologies and colour codings, but no transition branch was robust to strict versus dominant coding. Standardized observation therefore retained transition-class-dependent module reuse without one invariant molecular programme, while robust macroevolutionary pattern did not imply robust event identity. The molecular contrasts are not independent evolutionary origins, and the two analyses are not event-matched. These results distinguish what is repeatable at successive inferential targets without imputing missing molecular states or assigning causes to unstable branches.

## Introduction opening

What is actually repeatable when evolution repeatedly reaches a similar phenotype? A visible endpoint can recur while the mutation, gene, pathway module, whole molecular programme, realised phylogenetic pattern, or historical event does not. Evolutionary repeatability is therefore not one quantity. The empirical problem is to identify which level is supported by the observations at hand without treating incomplete molecular descriptions or reconstructed histories as direct samples of biological recurrence.

## Introduction closing

We tested two complementary boundaries in *Camellia*. First, we held five admitted public biological systems fixed and compared multivariate molecular recurrence under incomplete literature observation and one annotation-driven, outcome-independent A/F/C/P rule. Second, we held an accepted species set fixed and tested local wild-colour structure before applying a stricter topology- and colour-coding-sensitive gate to individual transition branches. The molecular contrasts quantify colour-state-generating transcript changes, not independent macroevolutionary origins; the two arms are joined by the question of what remains identifiable, not by event-for-event correspondence.

## Results order

1. Literature coverage and dependence collapse: 12 systems, six clusters, A/F/C/P 10/5/1/3 and 5/4/1/2, P=0.078125.
2. Anthocyanin-gain identified-set contraction under same-system standardized observation: three clusters; exact 0.333–1.0 to 0.333; pairwise 0.333–1.0 to 0.333–0.5; direct agreement 2/6.
3. Yellow-development modular recurrence: two trajectories; exact 0.5–1.0 to 0.5; pairwise 0.25–1.0 to 0.75; shared A down/C up/P down, differing F.
4. Accepted-species provenance and topology concordance: 53 shared tips, 46/50 splits shared, normalized RF=0.08.
5. Robust local nearest-same-colour structure: UFBoot strict P=0.00116, dominant P=0.0000800, with independent-topology support.
6. Event-identification stop: strict robust events=0, dominant=1 W→A, strict×dominant shared=0; no branch-specific ecology.

The current six-figure architecture is retained because each figure carries a distinct inferential gate: framework, molecular recurrence, taxonomy/evidence attrition, topology sensitivity, macro pattern, and event identity.

## Discussion logic

1. **Matched molecular observation result.** The same-system comparison quantifies how much multivariate recurrence remains identifiable after standardization; it does not claim that ascertainment effects are newly discovered.
2. **Bounded biological interpretation.** The result supports modular, transition-class-dependent repeatability in the observed systems, not a universal law from n=3 and n=2.
3. **Independent macro result.** A positive aggregate pattern and a failed branch-identity gate are allowed to coexist. This is not a novel ancestral-state theory.
4. **Why both arms matter.** Together they prevent one coarse phenotype label from licensing claims about a complete mechanism or a known historical origin. Their units and estimands remain separate.
5. **Claim ceiling.** Five of 53 accepted tips overlap direct taxon-level mechanistic evidence, three of 53 have immediately standardizable raw data, and zero of 53 have complete defensible A/F/C/P states. Micro-accessibility versus macro-transition probability is therefore untested.

## Editor-facing novelty statement

The study goes beyond reporting convergence in another taxon by quantitatively remeasuring the same public biological systems under a common multivariate pathway rule and by independently requiring aggregate macroevolutionary pattern and individual event identity to pass different robustness gates. The surviving novelty is this application-specific empirical combination; the constituent ideas and methods have substantial prior art.

## Cover-letter stress test

Dear Editors,

We submit “What repeats when evolution reaches similar flower colours in *Camellia*?” as an Original Article.

Repeated evolution is commonly discussed at multiple biological levels, but those levels are often treated as though they supplied interchangeable evidence. Our study provides two predeclared empirical tests. Holding five public biological systems constant, we show that a standardized, outcome-independent four-axis observation rule narrows literature-compatible molecular recurrence while preserving selected shared module directions. Holding an accepted taxon set constant, we show that local nearest-same-colour structure is robust across independent nuclear topologies and colour codings, while no individual transition branch survives the stronger strict-versus-dominant event gate.

This is not another report that similar colours can have different mechanisms. Nor do we claim a new theory of hierarchical convergence, measurement dependence, or ancestral-state uncertainty. The contribution is the quantified same-system observation intervention and the explicit separation of robust pattern from event identity in one frozen repeated-evolution study. The design exposes where empirical repeatability remains supported and where causal historical narration must stop.

All numerical results, code, data provenance, and inference ceilings are frozen and will be archived. The molecular systems are developmental or petal-sector transcript contrasts, not direct observations of independent evolutionary origins, and the molecular and macroevolutionary arms are not aligned event-for-event.

## Internal gate result

**Fail as first-submission route, but less decisively than the Letter route.** The same-system remeasurement is more than a simple replication in another taxon, and the six-figure structure is natural. However, the broad question is supported by a narrow application-specific combination of established ideas. The molecular arm has three and two independent units, the macro arm has a different unit and estimand, and 0/53 accepted taxa have complete A/F/C/P states. As a result, the cover letter's strongest honest novelty sentence still reads as a juxtaposition of two rigorous audits rather than a substantive general result about evolutionary predictability. Retargeting would increase desk-reject risk without increasing scientific truth or information.
