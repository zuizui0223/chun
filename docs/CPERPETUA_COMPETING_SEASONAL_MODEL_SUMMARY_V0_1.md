# C. perpetua seasonal model decision summary v0.1

Current historical anchors already show a large seasonal shift in reward and pollinator weighting. Therefore a winter-summer petal molecular difference alone is not sufficient evidence for sensory mediation, and a nonsignificant molecular test alone is not sufficient evidence for petal-state stability.

## Frozen historical magnitudes

- nectar volume winter/summer: 3.513x
- full-bloom sucrose/(glucose+fructose): 7.109x
- total nectar amino acids winter/summer: 0.402x
- sunbird visitation winter/summer: 2.90x
- Apis cerana visitation winter/summer: 0.241x
- A. mellifera ligustica visitation winter/summer: 0.186x
- bird/(two Apis) visitation weighting: approximately 13.76x more bird-weighted in winter

## Decision classes

1. Reward-only: reward/guild shift replicates **and a prespecified equivalence/SESOI gate excludes a biologically meaningful petal latent-state shift**.
2. Latent-state unresolved: Gate A is nonsignificant but equivalence is not established.
3. General seasonal physiology: petal latent state shifts, but bee-facing sensory direction and sensory incremental prediction fail.
4. Sensory+reward: latent state shifts, winter bee salience decreases, sensory state improves held-out pollinator-service prediction beyond reward/microclimate, and effective service improves reproductive prediction.
5. Behavior without fitness: sensory/service gates pass but effective service does not improve fruit/seed prediction.

Mixed cases remain unresolved.

## Power attack on the planned null

A deterministic exact-sign-flip simulation shows the 15-pair omnibus test has near-5% false-positive behavior but only about 41–54% power for a standardized multivariate shift norm of 0.8. Power rises to about 82–87% around a shift norm of 1.2. Thus failure to reject is not a stability result.

## Primary methodological protections

- plant is the independent unit;
- 15 paired plants -> exhaustive 32,768 whole-vector sign flips;
- A/F/C/P seasonal directions are not copied from the June developmental trajectory;
- equivalence bound must be set independently of the observed winter-summer mean;
- one primary Apis color-hexagon contrast is frozen before outcomes are examined;
- leave-one-plant-pair-out prediction holds winter and summer from the same plant out together;
- no post-hoc wavelength, metabolite, or gene can replace a failed primary gate.

This is an extant seasonal ecological-filter test only. Historical flower-colour transition causation remains outside the claim ceiling.
