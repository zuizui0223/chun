# Novelty and identifiability boundary v0.1

## Why the broad accessibility idea is not itself novel

The general proposition that short-timescale variation or developmental bias can align with deep macroevolution already has strong conceptual and empirical precedent.

Relevant anchors include:

- Jablonski 2020, *Developmental bias, macroevolution, and the fossil record*, DOI `10.1111/ede.12313`: explicitly separates the probability distribution of accessible variation from clade dynamics such as differential lineage survival and production.
- Tsuboi et al. 2024, *The paradox of predictability provides a bridge between micro- and macroevolution*, DOI `10.1093/jeb/voae103`: reviews and analyzes positive relationships between contemporary standing variation and macroevolutionary divergence.
- Cai, Melo & Des Marais 2025, *Disentangling variational bias: the roles of development, mutation, and selection*, DOI `10.1016/j.tig.2024.09.008`: emphasizes that different sources of variational bias must be defined and measured separately.
- Rohner & Berger 2025, *Macroevolution along developmental lines of least resistance in fly wings*, DOI represented by Nature article `s41559-025-02639-1`: directly shows that developmental/mutational variation in extant flies predicts deep dipteran wing divergence.
- Milocco 2026, *Mapping the possible: Developmental Coarse-Graining for Evolutionary Prediction*, DOI `10.1093/evolinnean/kzag023`: argues that evolutionary prediction requires timescale-appropriate representation of phenotypic accessibility.

Flower-colour evolution also already has strong pathway-level precedents:

- Smith et al. 2018, *Convergent Evolution at the Pathway Level: Predictable Regulatory Changes during Flower Color Transitions*, DOI `10.1093/molbev/msy117`;
- Wessinger et al. 2018, *Stepwise evolution of floral pigmentation predicted by biochemical pathway structure*, DOI `10.1111/evo.13589`.

Therefore this project must not claim novelty for `developmental accessibility can influence macroevolution` or `flower-colour pathways constrain transitions` in the abstract.

## Terminology correction

The current *Camellia* literature does **not** provide a standardized perturbation experiment or complete distribution of possible variants. It therefore does not identify a true variational-bias probability distribution.

Use these terms distinctly:

### Demonstrated short-timescale accessibility / feasibility

A state change has been observed within a genotype, developmental series, bud sport, or close cultivar system and can be generated without requiring deep species divergence.

This is an existence statement: `transition is demonstrably reachable`.

It is **not** an estimate of how frequently that transition is generated in nature.

### Variational bias

A probability distribution over phenotypic or mechanistic outcomes under a defined perturbation distribution.

This would require a denominator: for example, standardized random mutation, perturbation, segregating-variation, or comparable prospective sampling. Current *Camellia* literature cannot estimate this quantity.

### Literature ascertainment

The probability that a mechanistic axis is measured/reported conditional on a biological system entering the published literature.

The current analysis directly detects an anthocyanin-heavy ascertainment pattern at the biological-system level. Candidate-free public RNA-seq can reduce gene/axis ascertainment **within already studied systems**, but cannot by itself remove study-system publication bias.

### Macroevolutionary realization

The observed occupancy or concentration of a state across historical lineage backgrounds.

The current traditional-section analysis is a count-controlled historical-background proxy. It is not a branch transition rate.

### Persistence / clade dynamics

The processes determining whether generated states survive, spread, diversify, disappear or remain restricted after they arise.

These may include ecology, mating system, pleiotropy, introgression, extinction, lineage-specific opportunity and historical contingency.

## Current empirical novelty

The strongest result available from the present public-data architecture is not a generic accessibility claim. It is the explicit decomposition and empirical mismatch among three measured layers in one conserved floral metabolic system:

1. **demonstrated short-timescale feasibility** of multiple floral pigment outcomes;
2. **non-random literature ascertainment** of mechanistic axes, with anthocyanin strongly over-resolved at the system level;
3. **non-uniform macroevolutionary realization**, where A and Y outcomes occupy substantially fewer historical section backgrounds than expected from their species counts, whereas W does not.

The current result is therefore:

> **Demonstrated mechanistic feasibility is not equivalent to recurrent multivariate mechanism, and neither is equivalent to broad macroevolutionary realization.**

This is more defensible than claiming that short-timescale accessibility already predicts branch transition rates.

## What candidate-free RNA-seq adds

The candidate-free reanalysis is designed to remove one identifiable observation process:

`source-paper candidate choice -> which pathway axes appear recurrent`.

It does this by quantifying the same predefined A/F/C/P gene-family modules in every admitted raw dataset.

A positive result would show cross-system recurrence after reducing within-study candidate ascertainment.

A null result would show that the strong anthocyanin narrative in the published literature does not generalize to a common multivariate transition package.

Either result is publishable because the ascertainment problem is itself quantified before reanalysis.

## Remaining novelty target

The higher-value question, once the nuclear-tree layer becomes identifiable, is not simply `does micro predict macro?` but:

> **At which stage does predictability break: generation/feasibility, transition establishment, or long-term persistence?**

This directly motivates a sequence of nested models:

1. feasibility only;
2. feasibility + lineage/history;
3. feasibility + persistence proxies;
4. feasibility + ecology/reproductive-service context.

The present data already reject the simplest `feasible -> broadly realized` model at the historical-background level, but they do not yet identify the missing process.

## Forbidden claims

Do not claim that:

- current literature estimates the natural probability distribution of accessible colour states;
- short-timescale study counts are mutation or generation rates;
- traditional sections are branch-transition replicates;
- A/W/Y visible labels are biochemical states;
- the accessibility-realization gap proves ecological selection;
- candidate-free reanalysis removes publication bias between biological systems;
- the general idea that developmental accessibility shapes macroevolution is new.
