# Cross-radiation mechanistic-universe coverage audit — v0.1

## Why this audit exists

The tier-expanded resolution result could be misleading if the benchmark contains only clades selected because they happen to fit the emerging pattern.

This audit therefore asks a narrower question:

> **Within the pre-existing cross-clade atlas, were any mechanistically eligible clades omitted from the benchmark used to motivate the current synthesis?**

It does not claim that the atlas is a systematic census of all flower-colour literature.

## Temporal firewall

The relevant source universe predates the current resolution-specificity synthesis:

- `data/cross_clade_evidence_tier_registry_v0_5.csv` was added on **2026-09-06** in commit `1e69a6494ad9ec7e38b583158bb385ce8bd46296`;
- `data/cross_clade_mechanistic_recurrence_benchmark_v0_1.csv` was added minutes later on **2026-09-06** in commit `ac15ecfe1b1f390efbcead7683258671372b2bbe`;
- the present tier-expansion result was developed on **2026-09-13**.

Thus the atlas molecular-layer descriptions and the seven-system benchmark existed before the current `different mechanistic resolutions` interpretation.

This is not preregistration, but it is a useful post-outcome-selection firewall.

## v0.5 universe

The pre-existing evidence-tier registry contains **18 clades**.

Seven already had a molecular layer strong enough for the benchmark's recurrence-level classification, under the evidence roles recorded in that registry:

1. *Camellia*;
2. *Epimedium* sect. Diphyllon;
3. *Petunia* long-tube clade;
4. Cape *Erica*;
5. *Aquilegia*;
6. Iochrominae;
7. *Ipomoea* sect. *Astripomoea*.

All seven are present in the pre-existing mechanistic benchmark: **7/7 coverage, 0 eligible omissions**.

## Why the other 11 were not treated as recurrence-level benchmark systems

The exclusion categories reproduce the molecular-layer limitations already present in the v0.5 registry.

### Dependence collapse — 1 clade

- Antirrhineae: direct raw flower-colour RNA-seq is concentrated in the *Antirrhinum majus* Rosea/Eluta architecture; the strict independent-system gate had already failed.

### Network-confounded event comparison — 1 clade

- *Nicotiana*: multiple genomic/transcriptomic resources exist, but hybridization and polyploid reticulation complicate a clean independent-event recurrence denominator.

### Insufficient matched molecular layer — 9 clades

- *Hydrangea* sect. Cornidia — wild-section mechanism weak / cultivar-biased;
- Linoideae — molecular resources uneven and mostly crop-focused;
- *Polygonatum* sect. Verticillata — no direct pigment-network mechanism established;
- Angraecinae — molecular pigment layer weak;
- *Silene* Physolychnis — matched molecular evidence across independent red gains sparse;
- *Euonymus* — strong ecological evidence, weak floral-pigment molecular layer;
- *Linanthus* — molecular basis of polymorphism not established;
- *Acer* — comparative genetic-context evidence, no matched modern recurrence panel;
- *Dalechampia* — comparative pleiotropic context, no matched modern recurrence panel.

These are not biological negatives. They are unavailable or non-comparable for the recurrence-level estimand.

## Later prospective addition

Petunieae is deliberately kept outside the 18-clade v0.5 denominator because its 59/53-taxon matched phenotype-axis test was added later under a separate prospective gate.

Its role is stronger in a different sense: it is the only prospectively frozen M1 paired phenotype-axis test, and it returned hue PASS / amount FAIL-MIXED under the frozen primary responses.

## Result

Within the pre-existing v0.5 mechanistic universe:

- mechanistically eligible clades: **7**;
- included in benchmark: **7**;
- eligible omissions: **0**.

Therefore the current tier-expanded synthesis is **not produced by dropping an eligible v0.5 benchmark clade after seeing the resolution result**.

## What this does not solve

The atlas is curated from accessible comparative systems. It is not a PRISMA-complete or globally exhaustive survey of all flower-colour studies.

Residual risks remain:

- publication bias toward well-studied anthocyanin systems;
- availability bias toward clades with reusable expression/genetic data;
- taxonomic overrepresentation of classic evo-devo systems;
- heterogeneous observation regimes across clades.

Those risks require a broader literature-admission exercise; they cannot be erased by the 7/7 within-atlas coverage result.

## Consequence for the paper claim

The defensible statement becomes:

> **Within a cross-clade atlas whose mechanistic eligibility and seven-system benchmark predated the current synthesis, all eligible benchmark clades are retained. The observed resolution hierarchy therefore survives an explicit within-atlas coverage audit, although global literature-availability bias remains unresolved.**

Camellia Paper 1 remains unchanged and closed.
