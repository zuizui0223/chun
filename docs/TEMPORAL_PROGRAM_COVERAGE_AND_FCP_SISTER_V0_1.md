# CHUN temporal programme coverage and FCP sister framing — v0.1

## Decision

The current temporal programme is **broadly triangulated but not biologically exhaustive**.

It is close to comprehensive with respect to the main inferential failure modes already identified — prospective falsification, standardized cross-radiation comparison, temporal decay, nested hidden memory, structural-artifact checks, independent visible-colour replication, cross-representation tests, negative controls/failures and external nonexchangeable radiations.

It is **not** an exhaustive census of flowering plants, all flower-colour representations, or the ecological/developmental causes of memory loss.

The appropriate claim is therefore:

> **The programme tests the temporal organization of flower-colour history across multiple complementary evidence tiers; it establishes recurrent structure and bounded failures, not a universal law across all angiosperms or all phenotype representations.**

## 1. What has actually been tested

### A. Standardized visible-colour breadth

The Flower-clades-51 source provides the broad common frame.

- 51 predefined source clades were considered.
- 32 clades were eligible for direct fine-colour temporal-persistence analysis.
- 28 clades completed the standardized three-resolution profile.
- 21 clades had genuine fine-to-coarse opportunity for the hidden-memory test.
- 23 of the 51 source clades were held at the profile stage because the common-frame/state-variation gate was not satisfied; they were not rescued post hoc.

This is broad comparative coverage under one ontology and one analysis pipeline, but it is not a complete sample of angiosperm radiations.

### B. Prospective falsification

The preregistered Iris test directly challenged the proposed universal intermediate-resolution optimum and failed.

That failure is central evidence: the programme does not define a universal scale by choosing the best retrospective pattern.

### C. Temporal persistence

Among 32 eligible visible-colour clades:

- 23/32 had negative excess-retention slopes;
- median slope = -0.1897;
- 23/32 had positive integrated excess-retention area.

Thus exact visible colour generally retains more lineage-specific similarity at shallower relative divergence and loses that excess as divergence deepens.

The time axis is relative phylogenetic depth, not calibrated absolute time.

### D. Resolution heterogeneity

Among the 28 standardized completed clades, the profile classes include coarse, fine, tied and no-signal outcomes, with zero unique intermediate winners.

Four simple pre-outcome tree-geometry predictors failed qualification.

Therefore there is no supported universal privileged phenotype resolution in the current standardized frame.

### E. Hidden within-coarse history

Among 21 standardized radiations with genuine fine-to-coarse opportunity:

- 18/21 hidden-memory effects are positive;
- median centered effect = +0.0270 AUC;
- one-sided Wilcoxon P = 3.34e-5;
- sign-test P = 7.45e-4.

The amount of state-compression opportunity does not explain realization:

- rho = -0.2571;
- permutation P = 0.26023;
- all 21 leave-one-clade-out correlations are negative.

Thus recurrent hidden memory is not a trivial consequence of collapsing more fine states into fewer coarse states.

### F. Independent prospective visible-colour replication

Schistanthe provides the clean prospective visible-colour replication:

- 129 retained tips;
- centered effect = +0.06416 AUC;
- P = 0.0033;
- terminal PASS.

This is the strongest independent prospective support for the hierarchical-memory architecture.

### G. Cross-representation evidence

Biochemical representation does not yield a universal result.

- Petunieae: retrospective positive hidden-memory effect, +0.18794 AUC, P = 0.0001.
- Gesnerioideae: prospective FAIL, -0.00779 AUC, P = 0.5005.
- Ruellia: outcome remains unopened because the authoritative author-used tree is unavailable.

Therefore hidden memory is recurrent in visible colour but not established as a universal cross-representation law.

### H. External nonexchangeable radiations

Linoideae, Angraecinae and Antirrhineae each support finer organization after conditioning on a coarser state in source-specific analyses.

They are retained as three independent qualitative concordances, not pooled with the standardized AUC tests.

## 2. What is comprehensive, and what is not

### Comparatively comprehensive

The programme has deliberately tested:

1. whether a universal resolution optimum exists;
2. whether exact colour memory declines with divergence;
3. whether coarse states hide finer lineage history;
4. whether hidden memory is a coding/compression artifact;
5. whether global signal and hidden organization are equivalent;
6. whether hidden memory is merely a global fine-resolution advantage;
7. whether the architecture transports prospectively to an independent visible-colour radiation;
8. whether it generalizes to biochemical representations;
9. whether source-specific external radiations show analogous nested organization;
10. whether negative and HOLD outcomes remain visible rather than being silently replaced.

This is a broad **inferential-architecture test**.

### Not biologically exhaustive

The programme does not yet provide:

- an exhaustive taxonomic census across flowering plants;
- a random or probability sample of angiosperm radiations;
- complete representation coverage across visible colour, spectra, pigments, regulatory states and developmental modules;
- an established ecological cause of temporal memory decay;
- an established developmental or molecular cause of hidden-memory realization;
- a direct harmonized test connecting macroevolutionary memory to contemporary population-level spatial sorting;
- calibrated absolute-time decay constants shared across clades.

The bounded source-first replacement search also stopped after its frozen candidate budget rather than becoming an open-ended literature search. That is an anti-bias feature, not evidence of taxonomic exhaustiveness.

## 3. Sister relation to FCP

FCP is the geographic-space arm of the same programme.

The two projects are most naturally paired as follows:

| Axis | CHUN — evolutionary time | FCP — geographic space |
|---|---|---|
| Coordinate | relative evolutionary divergence | geographic distance / neighbourhood |
| Local organization | recent relatives retain excess colour similarity | nearby observations retain excess colour similarity |
| Heterogeneity | memory strength and carrying resolution vary among radiations | spatial organization and transition geography vary among species |
| Universal-template test | no universal privileged phenotype resolution | no confirmed universal global transition boundary |
| Nested structure | fine history can persist inside coarse phenotype classes | within-species variation can be locally structured despite no shared global map |
| Main causal gap | why memory decays / why hidden history is realized | why variation is locally maintained / geographically sorted |

The shared empirical architecture is:

> **Local or lineage-conditioned structure is recurrent, whereas one universal global template is not supported.**

In time, the local scale is phylogenetic proximity: recently diverged lineages retain more exact colour similarity, while the phenotype resolution carrying history differs among radiations.

In space, the local scale is geographic neighbourhood: nearby observations can be more colour-similar, while independent species do not share one confirmed universal geography of their strongest transitions.

This creates a coherent sister-paper programme:

> **Flower-colour variation is structured in both evolutionary time and geographic space, but the form of that structure is context dependent rather than governed by a single universal scale or map.**

## 4. General principle and its boundary

The strongest general principle currently justified is not that one mechanism or scale is universal.

It is:

> **Phenotypic organization can be recurrent without being templated universally: evolutionary history and geographic structure are retained locally, while the scale or location at which that structure is expressed varies among lineages and species.**

This is a programme-level synthesis, not a pooled statistical result.

Do not claim:

- that the same ecological mechanism causes both temporal and spatial structure;
- that temporal memory predicts FCP spatial sorting in the current datasets;
- that the 51-clade frame is an exhaustive sample of flowering plants;
- that hidden memory is universal across phenotype representations;
- that FCP and CHUN estimate the same parameter.

## Direct bridge feasibility audit

A direct CHUN–FCP coupling test was evaluated under an outcome-blind gate after the sister-paper framing was established.

The existing FCP discovery/validation cohorts were first checked only for taxonomic overlap with the 28 CHUN common temporal clades. Coverage was too sparse for a defensible radiation-level association:

- discovery: 7 overlapping clades / 17 D-eligible species;
- validation: 7 overlapping clades / 12 D-eligible species;
- only 1 validation clade contained at least 3 species.

No existing-outcome correlation was run.

A genuinely prospective bridge was then qualified using the untouched FCP U100 pool. The frozen gate required at least 5 unused high-depth species in at least 12 independent CHUN clades, with no threshold relaxation after seeing coverage.

After excluding every previously allocated high-depth cohort:

- unused U100 species = 2,730;
- exact-genus matches to the 28 CHUN common temporal clades = 127 species;
- clades with at least 5 unused species = 10;
- required qualifying clades = 12.

Terminal state:

> **HOLD_INSUFFICIENT_FRESH_CLADE_COVERAGE**

The threshold was not relaxed from 12 to 10, no synonym/family fallback was added, no fresh bridge cohort was selected, and no temporal-turnover/spatial-organization association was computed.

This means the temporal–spatial symmetry is presently a **programme-level empirical parallel**, not direct evidence that the same lineage property governs both axes.

## 5. Next genuinely new test

The clean next synthesis is not another retrospective moderator search.

It is a harmonized prospective test asking whether a predeclared property of a lineage predicts **both**:

1. faster loss of flower-colour memory across evolutionary divergence; and
2. stronger contemporary geographic turnover or spatial sorting within species.

That would turn the present sister-paper symmetry into a direct spatiotemporal ecological hypothesis.

Until such harmonized data exist, CHUN and FCP should remain analytically separate but explicitly framed as temporal and spatial sister papers.
