- within-*C. oleifera* replication: `ECO_BIRD_OLEIFERA_FRUIT`, `ECO_BEE_OLEIFERA_LIU2025_CAGE`;

The figure builder derives response ratios from registry numerator/denominator values when available and checks its geometric means against the regenerated v2 summary. Numeric values are not duplicated as script constants.

## Visual QA

The PNG render was inspected at full 3215 × 2011 resolution.

- all A–C panel titles, axes, species/state labels, effect annotations, and causal-boundary text are visible;
- no labels or arrows overlap critically or extend outside the canvas;
- the null RR = 1 references are visible in panels A and B;
- panel C distinguishes supported service/reliability effects from unresolved branch-specific historical causation;
- no sampling-error bars, formal pooled confidence interval, hue moderator effect, or direct abiotic causal effect is shown because those quantities are not supported by the current public data.

## Claim ceiling

Fig. 6 supports large, directionally repeatable reproductive-service effects across independent *Camellia* systems and climate/season mediation of pollination conditions. It does not show that service filtering caused a particular flower-colour transition, that bird service is specific to red/A flowers, or that direct abiotic pigment control is quantitatively replicated.
