## Temporal biological framing

Paper 1 asks:

> When similar flower-colour states are repeatedly generated through evolutionary time, how much of the underlying pigment-network transition is replayed?

The supported answer is that repeated visible generation does not replay one invariant complete A/F/C/P programme. Repeatability is modular and transition-class dependent. At the macro scale, realised wild colours remain locally phylogenetically structured, but individual historical transition branches remain unidentified under strict-versus-dominant trait sensitivity.
