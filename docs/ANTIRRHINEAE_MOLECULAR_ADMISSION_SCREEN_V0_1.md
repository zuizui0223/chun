# Antirrhineae molecular admission screen v0.1

## Verdict

**Strict molecular bridge gate: FAIL / HOLD.**

The tribe remains an exceptionally strong macroevolutionary and temporal–spatial polymorphism control, but the current public colour-specific molecular evidence is too concentrated in one *Antirrhinum majus* lineage to support a cross-tribe molecular transition-corridor analysis under the preregistered >=3 independent auditable-system rule.

## Auditable colour RNA-seq recovered

### GSE118621 / PRJNA486455

Corolla RNA-seq in *Antirrhinum majus* plants carrying Ros/El allele combinations associated with magenta versus Eluta floral-guide phenotypes. Raw SRA and processed Salmon expression are public. This is a strong colour-state system.

### PRJNA1054305

Nine raw RNA-seq experiments from the 2024 Eluta study (`10.1111/nph.19866`), comparing whole petals with nonsuppressive/suppressive Eluta genotypes and characterising anthocyanin-pathway repression. This is independently generated raw data, but it sits in the same *A. majus* lineage and related Rosea/Eluta regulatory architecture. It therefore cannot be treated as an independent long-term evolutionary transition replicate relative to GSE118621.

### PRJNA1290345

A 2025 BioProject describes an anthocyanin-deficient SIPPE50 versus anthocyanin-enriched JI2R transcriptomic comparison, but the current NCBI record reports no public linked raw data. It remains `HOLD`, not an auditable system.

## Tribe-outgroup searches

### Linaria vulgaris

Open flower RNA-seq exists:
- Darwin Tree of Life flower transcriptomes (`PRJEB50866`; flower RNA reads include ERR10378004/ERR10378005);
- comparative floral transcriptomics of nectar-spur development (`10.3389/fpls.2023.1190373`).

Neither provides a matched colour-state contrast, so neither satisfies the molecular recurrence endpoint.

Classical *Linaria* pigment chemistry documents anthocyanins and aurones across the genus (`10.1016/S0031-9422(00)85316-9`), supporting chemical comparability but not providing modern raw expression contrasts.

## Dependence collapse

Current count:
- raw direct colour contrasts: 2;
- both concentrated in the same *A. majus* lineage and overlapping regulatory architecture;
- raw direct colour contrasts outside *A. majus*: 0;
- public but currently unlinked candidate: 1;
- separate-genus floral transcriptomes lacking colour contrast: >=1.

Therefore the preregistered criterion of >=3 **independent auditable colour molecular systems** is not met.

## Consequence for the atlas

Antirrhineae should be **demoted from proposed molecular anchor to macro/temporal–spatial control**.

Its unique value remains:
- 369-species colour survey;
- 169-taxon phylogeny;
- explicit current colour polymorphism;
- correspondence between the most frequent current polymorphism and the most frequent long-term transition class;
- open CC0 trait/tree bundle.

The molecular-anchor priority moves to **Epimedium sect. Diphyllon**, where a white plesiomorphic state, repeated multi-colour evolution and an eight-species anthocyanidin/gene-expression comparison already coexist.

## Reopen condition

Reopen the Antirrhineae molecular bridge only if one of the following occurs:
1. >=1 additional colour-contrast raw dataset from another Antirrhineae genus becomes available and the total reaches >=3 defensibly independent systems;
2. PRJNA1290345 raw reads are released and a second non-overlapping lineage system is also recovered;
3. a new cross-species molecular study directly measures matched yellow/white/anthocyanin states across multiple Antirrhineae taxa.

Until then, do not weaken the independence rule merely to make the candidate pass.

Paper 1 remains unchanged and closed.
