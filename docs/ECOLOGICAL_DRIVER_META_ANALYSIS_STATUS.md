# Ecological-driver meta-analysis — Paper 1 integration complete at v2

The ecological-driver layer has completed its current public-data synthesis and is no longer an open-ended search gate.

## Quantitative result

The strongest repeatable ecological signal is reproductive-service / reliability filtering.

- Cross-species bird-access fruit-set effects span A, W and Y visible states and are positive in 3/3 independent species; equal-weight geometric mean RR = 3.53 (range 2.29–6.35; leave-one-out 2.64–4.39).
- Within *Camellia oleifera*, independent bird-exclusion and *Apis cerana* introduction experiments both show large fruit-set benefits (RR = 2.29 and 2.56; geometric mean RR = 2.42). A secondary open-field honey-bee contrast gives RR = 4.31.
- Five registered pollinator-reliability gradient effects all point in the expected direction: higher legitimate-bee availability predicts greater fruit set / lower pollen limitation, and greater distance from a nesting aggregation predicts lower fruit set.
- Climate/season mediation is represented by five studies across four taxa and repeatedly alters flowering phenology, visitor activity, reward, pollen delivery or reproductive service.
- Direct abiotic floral-pigment evidence remains limited to one *Camellia* experiment in which cold and darkness are confounded; no formal direct-abiotic floral meta-analysis is justified.
- Same-visible-red flowers can differ >20-fold in bumblebee visitation, so A/W/Y is not a deterministic ecological-state variable.

## Interpretation boundary

The ecological synthesis supports a conditional filtering model at the mechanism/service level:

`molecular accessibility -> latent pigment/spectral/reward phenotype -> flowering-window environment + pollinator availability/effectiveness -> reproductive success -> persistence`.

It does **not** establish that these ecological effects caused particular accepted-species flower-colour transitions, because no transition branch is robust to both strict and dominant wild-colour scenarios after taxonomy/polymorphism audit.

## Paper 1 consequence

The manuscript should no longer state simply that ecological causes are untestable. It should distinguish:

1. **mechanism/service level:** quantitative support for pollinator-service and reliability filtering, with climate/season mediation;
2. **macroevolutionary event level:** branch-specific causal attribution remains unidentifiable from current public data;
3. **direct abiotic pigment level:** current flower-specific experiments are too sparse/confounded for a comparable synthesis.

The molecular-accessibility and accepted-species macro-pattern results remain frozen. The next task is manuscript/abstract integration, not further indiscriminate ecological searching.
