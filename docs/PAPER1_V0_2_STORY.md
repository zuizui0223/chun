# Paper 1 v0.2 story — observation regime, modular recurrence, and macro realization

## One-sentence thesis

> **Repeated flower-colour change in Camellia does not imply recurrence of one invariant pigment-state package: outcome-independent remeasurement removes literature-permitted whole A/F/C/P recurrence in both red/pink gain and yellow development, reveals transition-class-dependent modular reuse, and remains compatible with robust local phylogenetic colour structure even when individual historical events are not identifiable.**

## Why v0.1 needs restructuring

The v0.1 manuscript was organized around sequence-level examples of molecular flexibility (FLS same-lineage recurrence and DFR paralog substitution) followed by macro colour conservatism. Those examples remain valid, but they no longer carry the strongest molecular evidence.

The new candidate-free analysis directly measures the observation process on the same public biological systems. It therefore changes the paper from:

`multiple molecular routes -> macro conservatism`

into:

`feasibility -> observation regime -> whole-package versus modular recurrence -> macro realization -> event-identifiability boundary`.

FLS/DFR copy-level examples should move to Supplement/background support. The candidate-selected versus candidate-free comparison becomes the Main molecular result.

## Primary questions

1. **Observation:** Does mechanistic recurrence inferred from selected literature survive one frozen pathway-wide A/F/C/P remeasurement of the same public systems?
2. **Recurrence scale:** Is evolutionary repetition better described as recurrence of a complete pigment-state package or reuse of only some pathway modules?
3. **Macro realization:** Are visible flower-colour states nevertheless non-randomly distributed on accepted-species nuclear topologies?
4. **Identification boundary:** Can individual transition branches and their ecological causes be identified robustly from current public hard-state data?

## Results order

### R1. The literature observation regime is incomplete and anthocyanin-heavy

Retain the exact ascertainment result as the opening empirical motivation: biological-system coverage A/F/C/P = 8/4/1/3; conditional A-enrichment P=0.00836 before dependence collapse and 0.140625 after collapse. The key message is not that A is biologically dominant; it is that the literature measurement process is nonuniform.

### R2. Candidate-free remeasurement removes complete anthocyanin whole-package recurrence

Three independent clusters: `CJAPONICA`, `CRETICULATA`, `CSIN_WHITE_PINK`.

- literature exact-signature recurrence: 0.333–1.0;
- candidate-free exact-signature recurrence: 0.333 exactly;
- literature pairwise concordance: 0.25–1.0;
- candidate-free: 0.333–0.5;
- identified-set width: 0.75 -> 0.1667;
- directly comparable literature/candidate-free cells: 2/5 agreement.

Interpretation: selected literature leaves complete multivariate recurrence admissible, but standardized measurement does not. Do not call CJAPONICA A a strong reversal: g=-0.0168 is effectively near-flat.

### R3. Yellow development retains strong modular recurrence but not an exact whole package

Two independently frozen ordered trajectories:

- `CNITIDISSIMA`: A-/F+/C+/P-;
- `CPERPETUA`: A-/F-/C+/P-.

Observed pre-registered branch: 3/4 axes match, only F differs.

- literature exact-signature recurrence: 0.5–1.0;
- candidate-free: 0.5 exactly;
- literature pairwise concordance: 0.25–1.0;
- candidate-free: 0.75 exactly;
- width: 0.75 -> 0;
- direct literature/candidate-free agreement: 4/5.

This is the critical control against a generic pipeline-reversal story. Candidate-free measurement mostly agrees with literature in yellow systems but still changes the identified whole-package conclusion because it fills missing axes.

### R4. Accepted-taxonomy and wild-colour audits reduce trait certainty

Retain 93 legacy tips -> 55 accepted species and 35 provisional hard states -> strict 24 / dominant 30. This establishes that the macro trait layer has its own observation process and cannot be treated as truth labels.

### R5. Local visible-colour conservatism survives topology and trait sensitivity

Retain nearest-same-colour result on both nuclear topologies. Headline UFBoot values: strict P=0.00116; dominant P=0.000080. Global MPD and A-specific permissivity remain demoted.

### R6. Pattern is identifiable without identifiable events

Retain strict robust branches=0, dominant=1, strict×dominant shared=0. This is the macro analogue of the molecular identification argument: a pattern can be robust while individual event identities and causes are not.

## Discussion structure

### D1. Repeated phenotype is not repeated whole mechanistic state

The central biological result is the separation of whole-package and modular recurrence. Anthocyanin-gain systems are heterogeneous; yellow systems reuse A/C/P directions while differing at F. Evolutionary repeatability therefore depends on the level at which mechanism is represented.

### D2. Observation regime is part of the inferential object

The paper should not frame literature studies as “wrong.” Candidate-selected and pathway-wide protocols answer different questions. The empirical contribution is to show how changing the observation regime changes the identified recurrence space on the same biological systems.

### D3. Molecular feasibility and macro realization remain different quantities

Sequence-level FLS/DFR evidence demonstrates feasibility/implementation plurality, but it should now support rather than define the main molecular argument. The macro nearest-neighbour pattern says realised visible states remain locally history-dependent despite mechanistic modularity.

### D4. Pattern without events

The shared conceptual result across scales is identification. Molecular whole-package recurrence is not identified by selected literature alone; macro event branches are not identified by current species-level colour states. More model complexity cannot recover information that was never observed.

### D5. Ecology belongs at persistence/filtering, not branch assignment

Retain pollination/climate screens as context and Supplement. Do not make visible hue a deterministic ecological state or assign pollinator/climate causes to branches that fail the event-identifiability gate.

## Abstract core

Premise: repeated phenotypes are often interpreted as evidence for recurrent mechanisms, but selected molecular studies observe only subsets of pathway state.

Methods: remeasure five public Camellia RNA-seq systems using one frozen A/F/C/P protocol; compare exact partially identified recurrence under literature versus candidate-free observation; independently test accepted-species nuclear colour structure and event identifiability.

Results: candidate-free measurement fixes anthocyanin exact-signature recurrence at 1/3 and yellow at 1/2; complete whole-package recurrence is removed from both admissible sets. Yellow retains 3/4 module agreement, showing modular recurrence rather than no recurrence. Wild visible colour remains locally phylogenetically clustered, but no transition branch is robust to strict versus dominant wild-colour coding.

Conclusion: mechanistic recurrence, observation regime, and macroevolutionary realization are distinct quantities. Repeated flower colour can reflect class-specific reuse of pathway modules without recurrence of one complete molecular package, and robust macro patterns need not identify their individual generating events.
