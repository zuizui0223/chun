# Cross-radiation representation admission — v0.1

**Status:** completed source-document audit, **not** a new empirical multilayer test. The question is whether the CHUN temporal atlas has enough matched systems to claim that phylogenetic gene-expression history predicts biochemical products differently across independent radiations.

## Decision

The existing standardized visible-colour evidence is broad but not a transcript-metabolite panel: 51 predefined source clades, 32 fine-colour temporal slopes, 28 standardized resolution profiles, 21 hidden-memory-eligible clades, and independent prospective Schistanthe validation. That supports **recurrent visible-colour nested phylogenetic history**, not a 32-clade molecular mechanism test.

**Only Petunieae is currently source-verified as a same-tip dated tree + quantitative floral pigment compounds + 21 measured gene-expression columns, on 47 retained taxa.** Its original fixed four flavonol-related genes show positive nearest-relative expression transfer (+5.61%), while nearest-relative flavonol concentration transfer is negative (−24.08%). The source class-only direct expression→product predictor improved +6.42%, but a fixed ancestry covariance made the additional gene gain −4.21% (model P=.3439), and a training-only fitted covariance gave −6.43% (post-outcome, no new P). These are distinct retrospective within-radiation estimands.

Prospective Gesnerioideae is a valid **biochemical hidden-memory FAIL**, not a same-tip transcript-metabolite validation. Ruellia's author-used source tree is unavailable and its biochemical outcome remains HOLD. Camellia provides heterogeneous molecular/developmental mechanism evidence but not a verified second same-tip quantitative 47-like expression/metabolite panel. Iris, Schistanthe and the other source-specific external radiations should not be relabelled as molecular replicates.

## Admitted versus unavailable tiers

| Evidence layer | Current source coverage | Inference permitted |
|---|---|---|
| Visible-colour temporal retention | 32 eligible clades | 23/32 negative relative-time slopes; no universal decay rate |
| Visible-colour conditional hidden history | 21 opportunity clades; independent Schistanthe prospective PASS | Recurrent hidden history, not universal across representations |
| Biochemical hidden history | Petunieae retrospective positive, Gesnerioideae prospective FAIL, Ruellia HOLD | Mixed source-specific results, not a universal chemical rule |
| Matched transcript + metabolite + dated tree | **Petunieae 1 radiation** | Within-radiation descriptive/predictive source effects only |
| Pollinator interactions, UV reflectance, fitness linked to matched molecular layers | Not verified in the current panel | **No causal ecological mechanism claim** |

The complete, inspectable per-system admission ledger is `results/cross_radiation_representation_admission_v0_1.json`. Its statuses mean **not established from the currently inspected source documentation**, not proof that data cannot exist in future publications.

## Decision for the integrated manuscript

Keep the strong cross-radiation story focused on **visible-colour temporal persistence and hidden fine-state phylogenetic organization**. Use Camellia as a heterogeneous mechanism case, and Petunieae as a particularly well measured **single-radiation test showing that detectable molecular history does not guarantee transferable chemical-product prediction**. Explicitly retain the prospective biochemical failure and the phylogenetic-model sensitivity. Do not claim 32 molecular replicates or a common rate of memory decay from these datasets.

The next genuinely new empirical step requires one **independent source-verified radiation** with identical tip identifiers across dated tree, quantitative metabolites and floral transcript expression. Freeze its source/taxon/predictor/phenotype gates before observing the target contrast; otherwise report it as retrospective. Until such a source is found, expanding analyses within Petunieae cannot substitute for cross-radiation replication.
