# Evolution Letters v0.3 hostile novelty audit — 2026-09-19

## Question

Does the temporal-memory reframing create a stronger biological paper, or does it accidentally repackage prior flower-color phylogenetic-signal work as a new discovery?

## Prior-art finding

The existence of phylogenetic structure in flower color is established prior art.

Examples:
- McEwen & Vamosi (2010), DOI 10.1098/rspb.2010.0501, explicitly measured phylogenetic signal in floral color and found flower color labile with weak signal in a subalpine community.
- Shrestha et al. (2014), DOI 10.1111/1365-2745.12185, tested whether flower-color similarity is a function of evolutionary relatedness using phylogenetic-signal statistics and color-distance versus phylogenetic-distance comparisons.
- Skeels et al. (2021), Evolution Letters, DOI 10.1002/evl3.225, analyzed macroevolutionary flower-color signal/rates and ecological interactions in Hakea.

Therefore CHUN must not claim novelty for:
- flower color having phylogenetic signal;
- close relatives being more similar in flower color;
- flower color being evolutionarily labile;
- converting phylogenetic distance into a generic statement that similarity decays with divergence.

## Retained novelty

The defensible novelty is the combination of:

1. **Prospective falsification of a resolution rule.**  
   The intermediate-resolution optimum was frozen before the independent Iris outcome and failed.

2. **Standardized cross-radiation resolution test.**  
   Under one common ontology/estimator across 51 clades, 28 completed profiles contain coarse, fine, tied and no-signal outcomes, with zero unique intermediate winners.

3. **No universal privileged phenotypic scale.**  
   The same evolutionary history can be more or less visible depending on phenotype representation, but the informative representation is not consistently coarse, intermediate or fine across radiations.

4. **Temporal interpretation, not independent evidence.**  
   The 32-clade persistence curve and 28-clade half-depth analysis translate the same pairwise phylogenetic information into a biological divergence-depth language. They strengthen interpretation but do not increase independent replication.

5. **Cross-representation boundary.**  
   Petunieae shows that fine biochemical composition can retain strong structure when coarse summaries do not, but one biochemical exact-profile unit is insufficient to claim a general biochemical rule.

## Editorial implication

The manuscript should lead with the unresolved general problem:

> At what biological scale is evolutionary history most recoverable from a phenotype, and is that scale predictable across independent radiations?

The positive time-axis result should answer what the AUC means biologically:

> flower-color history is transient across relative divergence.

It should not be presented as the principal novelty.

## Current title decision

Preferred candidate:

**Flower-color evolutionary memory is transient but lacks a universal phenotypic scale**

This keeps the biological time axis visible while making the cross-scale result the nontrivial part of the claim.

## Claim ceiling

Allowed:
- exact flower-color state sharing generally declines across relative divergence in the standardized batch;
- this is consistent with transient evolutionary memory;
- the phenotypic resolution that best retains same-state structure is heterogeneous among radiations;
- no universal coarse/intermediate/fine optimum is supported;
- the intermediate-optimum prediction was prospectively falsified.

Not allowed:
- first demonstration that flower color has phylogenetic signal;
- first demonstration that flower-color similarity declines with evolutionary distance;
- persistence curves as independent replication of the AUC;
- absolute persistence duration in Ma;
- ecological causation from the current memory-decay analysis.

## Decision

Keep v0.3 as the biologically stronger candidate, but only under this novelty ceiling. v0.2 remains preserved as the safer representation-centered fallback.
