# WFO55 wild-colour audit gate

## Purpose

The Fan-derived colour labels are historical hard labels, not evidence of wild monomorphism. After WFO 2026-06 taxonomy collapse, every accepted species with any Fan colour observation is re-audited against species-level wild/floristic descriptions before ancestral-colour or branch-transition inference is allowed.

## Frozen decisions

- WFO accepted Camellia species in the nuclear sensitivity backbone: 55.
- Accepted species with any Fan colour evidence (including the taxonomy-induced C. brevistyla conflict): 36.
- Provisional single hard states before the wild-colour audit: 35.
- Strict wild/floristic seed: 24 species (A=1, W=19, Y=4).
- Dominant-colour sensitivity seed: 30 species (A=4, W=22, Y=4).
- Eleven provisional hard labels are demoted from the strict seed because wild evidence is polymorphic, dominant-only/ontogenetically qualified, or exact-species colour evidence is insufficient.

The strict seed is deliberately conservative. Examples include C. japonica and C. saluenensis (wild A/W polymorphism), C. cuspidata (white or pale red), C. reticulata/C. polyodonta/C. subintegra (pigmented-dominant but rare white), C. sasanqua and C. granthamiana (white-dominant with pink tint), and C. crispula/C. gaudichaudii (insufficient exact colour evidence in the audited sources).

## Next gate

Rebuild accepted-species colour history and branch transition posteriors on the WFO55 nuclear tree under both the strict and dominant seeds. A macro transition is allowed into branch-climate, pollination, or micro-to-macro causal tests only if its direction is robust to both trait scenarios and the topology sensitivity used downstream.

## Claim ceiling

This gate audits trait evidence. It does not infer ancestral state, transition rate, adaptation, or ecological causation.
