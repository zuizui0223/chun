# Evolution escalation after Petunieae — v0.1

Checked: 2026-09-11

## Decision

**Evolution escalation remains HOLD; AJB is not yet displaced as the authorized Paper 1 first-submission route.**

The evidence has materially strengthened since the 2026-08-28 journal-escalation gate, because the programme now contains two independent retrospective matched-definition cross-level alignments and one genuinely pre-specified prospective Petunieae test. However, the prospective test is `PETUNIEAE_PROSPECTIVE_BRIDGE_MIXED`, not PASS. It therefore does not yet supply the clean independent prospective replication needed to convert the cross-level pattern into a general evolutionary claim.

This decision does not downgrade the Petunieae result. Its hue-axis component is a strong positive prospective result.

## What changed after the old AJB-versus-Evolution gate

The old Paper 1 gate was written before the external-radiation cross-level programme. The later evidence now separates three levels:

1. **Camellia Paper 1:** molecular repeatability is modular and transition-class dependent; visible colour is locally phylogenetically structured; individual historical event identity is weak.
2. **External macro radiations:** fine-state phylogenetic organization recurs, whereas no tested coarse boundary or transition direction is universally privileged.
3. **Cross-level bridges:** visible phenotype dimensions can align with different molecular subspaces within radiations.

Under the matched bridge definition `VISIBLE_PHENOTYPE_AXIS_TO_MOLECULAR_SUBSPACE_WITHIN_ONE_RADIATION`:

- Iochrominae = retrospective alignment;
- Cape Erica = retrospective alignment;
- Petunieae = first prospectively specified test, classification MIXED;
- Aquilegia = HOLD because the matched two-axis direct molecular observation regime was incomplete;
- Epimedium = complementary different-definition case, not pooled with the matched bridge.

Thus the old statement that the molecular and macro material consists only of two non-matched audits is stale. There is now a real cross-radiation bridge programme. The remaining question is whether that bridge has enough prospective replication to carry a general-evolution manuscript.

## Petunieae result relevant to journal escalation

### Strong positive component

For 53 anthocyanin-positive Petunieae taxa, the pre-specified hue/hydroxylation axis selected `BRANCHING_HUE`:

- best AICc = 26.61293763;
- next-best AICc = 38.21576699;
- margin = 11.60282936;
- null AICc = 38.34002462.

This is a strong prospective alignment of a visible biochemical hue dimension with a branching/hydroxylation molecular subspace.

### Failed full-bridge component

For the pre-specified raw total-anthocyanin amount axis across 59 taxa:

- best subspace = `BRANCHING_HUE`;
- best-vs-next margin = 0.44436806;
- best AICc = 120.99361154;
- null AICc = 120.93638460.

The amount axis therefore does not provide the predicted distinct late/regulatory subspace under the frozen response definition.

The source-method log-amount sensitivity selects `LATE_OUTPUT` with a 2.80766099 AICc margin and a 6.87691264-unit improvement over null, but this transform was not the preregistered primary response and cannot upgrade the gate.

## What can now be claimed beyond the old AJB package

A defensible programme-level statement is now:

> **The molecular subspace associated with floral colour variation depends on the phenotype dimension being resolved. Hue/hydroxylation repeatedly concentrates on pathway-branching machinery across independent radiations, including a strong prospectively specified Petunieae test, whereas the molecular localization of pigment amount/depletion is less stable and can depend on response definition.**

This is stronger and more general than the original Camellia-only AJB framing, but it is not equivalent to:

> every visible phenotype axis maps reproducibly to a distinct molecular subspace.

The latter is rejected by the frozen Petunieae primary amount result.

## Why Evolution is still HOLD

A move to *Evolution* should require a contribution that is not mainly a juxtaposition of established principles or a response-definition-sensitive extension. The new programme has moved closer to that threshold, but four constraints remain:

1. **Prospective full-bridge replication count is zero.** Petunieae is MIXED.
2. **The cleanest prospective result is one component, hue → branching machinery.** This is biologically interesting but narrower than the two-axis generalization tested by the v0.5 bridge.
3. **The amount/depletion result is transform-sensitive.** The source-method log response agrees with the retrospective expectation, but the pre-frozen raw response does not.
4. **Paper 1 itself remains Camellia-only.** The external-radiation evidence is a later programme layer and should not be silently retrofitted into the frozen AJB bundle without a deliberate new manuscript architecture.

Therefore the existing AJB manuscript should remain frozen while the programme seeks one decisive prospective replication.

## Decisive next gate

Search for one additional independent radiation that satisfies all of the following **before inspecting its molecular result distribution**:

- >=30 matched taxa if possible;
- a direct, source-defined amount/depletion phenotype axis;
- a direct, source-defined hue/hydroxylation or comparable branching phenotype axis;
- matched expression or functional molecular measurements in the same radiation;
- a usable branch-length phylogeny;
- enough frozen pathway variables to test at least three predeclared molecular subspaces.

Before source-value inspection, freeze the phenotype definitions, molecular subspaces, response transforms, phylogenetic model, and PASS/MIXED/FAIL threshold.

### Escalation rule

- **If a second prospective radiation gives full two-axis PASS:** rerun the journal gate with *Evolution* as the preferred first target and build a new general-evolution manuscript around cross-radiation phenotype-resolution → molecular-subspace predictability. Do not simply rename the current AJB manuscript.
- **If it gives MIXED/FAIL:** retain AJB for Camellia Paper 1 and treat the cross-radiation result as a separate paper whose central result is dimension-specific and partially non-universal molecular mapping.
- **Do not use post hoc response transformations to convert MIXED into PASS.**
