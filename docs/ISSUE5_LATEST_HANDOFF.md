# Issue 5 latest handoff — 2026-08-15

## New chun result

Count-controlled traditional-section concentration on the exact 50-species screen (100,000 state-label shuffles preserving A/W/Y sample sizes):

- A: 14 taxa, 2 sections; expected breadth 6.59279; lower-tail P=0.00003; entropy lower-tail P<0.00001.
- W: 34 taxa, 9 sections; expected breadth 9.12673; breadth P=0.651; entropy P=0.413.
- Y: 2 taxa, 1 section; underpowered.

## Consequence

Raw W 9/10 section occupancy is not evidence that white is an unusually broad evolutionary hub once sample count is controlled. The striking signal is the opposite asymmetry: anthocyanin-like A is molecularly recurrent/easy to deploy but is macro-persistent in very few historical sections.

## New primary problem

> Why does a molecularly accessible pigment state become persistent only in particular lineage/ecological backgrounds?

## Hypothesis priority

1. H_LINEAGE_PERMISSIVITY — strongest new cross-scale hypothesis.
2. H_SELECTION_TARGET — selection may act on biochemical/spectral latent state rather than W/A/Y hue.
3. H_REACT_ORDER — environment-first/follower versus reactivation-first/enabler requires branch timing.
4. H_CLIMATE_POLL_MED — climate may act indirectly through pollinator reliability/seasonality.
5. H_ECOLOGICAL_RECURRENCE — some regain may restore an ancestral-like ecological signal rather than enable a novel niche.
6. Historical/neutral recurrence remains a strong null/background model.
