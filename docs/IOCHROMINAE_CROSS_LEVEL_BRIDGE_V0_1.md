# Iochrominae cross-level bridge v0.1

## Result

Iochrominae provides a same-radiation bridge between visible flower-colour representation and molecular resolution, using the 28-species source study `10.1093/molbev/msy117`.

The bridge is not a raw reanalysis of the unavailable Dryad bytes. It is a source-derived, machine-readable synthesis of results explicitly reported in the article and supplementary material.

### Phenotype dimension 1 — anthocyanin presence/intensity

Four independent losses of ancestral delphinidin pigmentation are associated with repeated strong down-regulation of the late pathway module `F3'5'H + DFR + ANS`. Early genes `CHS + CHI + F3H` remain comparatively conserved across pigment states.

### Phenotype dimension 2 — anthocyanidin hue

Transitions among delphinidin, cyanidin and pelargonidin are associated primarily with the branching-gene subspace `F3'H + F3'5'H`, rather than the whole pathway.

### Regain

`Iochroma edule`, nested in an unpigmented context, shows elevated late-module expression relative to unpigmented relatives, but one extant regain tip is insufficient for a general regain law.

## Interpretation

Within one radiation, different visible phenotype dimensions map to different molecular subspaces. This is compatible with the merged state-granularity result: collapsing flower colour to one coarse representation can merge biologically distinct molecular routes.

It does **not** establish that visible-state granularity is caused by these molecular modules, nor that the same module mapping recurs across clades. It also does not identify a universal exact gene, mutation, ecological driver or transition direction.
